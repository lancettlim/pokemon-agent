# Data terms

The code is MIT-licensed (see `LICENSE`). The dataset in `releases/` and the
dashboard's data are described here.

## Dataset tables

The CSV tables are our own normalization, mapping and aggregation work and
are offered under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/): reuse them,
including commercially, with attribution to this project. Every row carries
`source_name`, `source_url` and `source_record_id`; the facts underneath
come from the upstream sources listed in `docs/data-sources.md` and remain
subject to those sources' own terms. Check them before redistributing at
scale.

## Artwork

Pokémon names, sprites and artwork are the property of Nintendo, Game
Freak, Creatures Inc. and The Pokémon Company. Sprite images in the dataset
and on the dashboard are sourced from Bulbagarden Archives. They are **not**
relicensed by this project and are included for identification only. If you
redistribute them, you do so under your own responsibility. A rights holder
who wants an image removed can open an issue and it will be taken down.

## Not affiliated

This is an unofficial fan project, not affiliated with or endorsed by
Nintendo, Game Freak, The Pokémon Company or Play! Pokémon.

## Player names

Tournament results use the names the tournaments publish. Online handles
are never matched to real names, and Showdown replay player names are not
stored. To have a record corrected or removed, open a data-correction issue.
